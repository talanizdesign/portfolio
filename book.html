Emotion-Driven Innovation

Emotion-Driven Innovation is a research-based method for applyting emotions into product strategy

Most product teams are very good at making things work. Far fewer can explain why some products get loved while others just get used. The book addresses that gap with three tools.

First, a precise vocabulary of positive emotions. Teams stop saying "delight" for everything and start naming what they actually mean: relief, pride, fascination, confidence.

Second, a model of when emotions happen: at first impression, during use, and in what the product says about the person who chooses it.

Third, a process that turns that understanding into concrete design decisions.

Why this Matters now

AI is making functional design faster and cheaper. When every team can ship a competent interface in a fraction of the time, competence stops being what sets a product apart. The ability to understand what people feel, and to design for it responsibly, stays human. That is where the difference will come from.
