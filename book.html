Emotion-Driven Innovation

Emotion is one of the most powerful, and most underestimated, drivers of product success. But how do designers actually prepare to convey emotions through what they create? How do they know which choices will provoke the right response?

My book, published by Springer in 2024, tackles those questions head-on. Grounded in my doctoral research and real-world practice, it offers structured frameworks for integrating emotional insight into the design process in a systematic and strategic way.

From driving product innovation to sharpening market differentiation, the book gives design professionals tools to work with emotion intentionally, rather than by instinct alone.
