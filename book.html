Emotion-Driven Innovation

Design is not just about how interfaces look; it is about how they govern human behavior. I co-authored Emotion-Driven Innovation to transform human psychology from a subjective creative byproduct into a predictable asset that organizations can intentionally engineer.

Rooted in a precise vocabulary of 19 positive emotions, this methodology replaces creative guesswork with clear "emotional jobs to be done." It gives cross-functional teams the exact structural tools required to translate positive human emotions into measurable product adoption and long-term user retention.

Why this Matters for Digital Products & Advanced AI

The translation of E-DI into the digital product realm addresses a critical operational challenge: the systemic elimination of cognitive friction.

In highly complex software, enterprise dashboards, and high-performance digital platforms, user drop-off rarely stems from a lack of features. Instead, it happens due to emotional misalignment, users feel overwhelmed, unconfident, or frustrated by legacy structural patterns.

By applying the precise positive emotions to digital product briefs, teams can treat user psychology as a measurable data layer. By explicitly mapping the "emotional job to be done" across the interface, we establish strict visual and behavioral guidelines that directly insulate development velocity.

In an era where AI can build code at zero marginal cost, the strategic premium belongs to the teams who can decode human behavior and turn qualitative emotional targets into quantitative product adoption.
