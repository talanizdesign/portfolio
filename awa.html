awa

The idea

Awa tracks emotional impact. It introduces a qualitative feedback loop to digital well-being by prompting people to map specific positive or negative emotions to their usage.

Over time, it surfaces clear behavioural patterns, so people can audit their digital habits based on underlying sentiment rather than just minutes.

For businesses, it unlocks anonymised, aggregate sentiment telemetry, helping teams evaluate product-market resonance without compromising individual privacy.

Where it comes from

Where it comes from

Awa is the productisation of my research in Emotion-Driven Innovation (published via Springer, 2024).

The underlying framework shifts emotion from an accidental byproduct of UX to an intentional, measurable design metric.

Awa is the live execution of this theory: moving past raw behavioural analytics to capture explicit emotional vectors.

Formulating Awa's product intent

I collaborated with brand-identity designer to codify a clear brand strategy, establishing an intentional point of view.

Tap a pillar to explore it.

Mission

Help people make decisions about the apps they use, based on their wellbeing.

Target audience

People who want to understand the emotions the digital products they pay for or use provoke, and make more meaningful product choices.

Core values

Honest Empathetic Agency-preserving Private by design

Tone of voice

Friendly, not cheerful Conversational, not chatty Non-judgmental, not neutral Minimal, not clinical

The vibe

Approachable, helpful and human, turning messy, everyday feelings into calm, readable insights through fluid data particles, semantic colour themes and a clean, low-stimulus timeline.
